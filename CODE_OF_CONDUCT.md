# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project's issues, pull requests, and discussions to be a place
people actually want to bring bug reports, questions, and contributions.
Everyone participating — maintainers included — is expected to help keep it
that way.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Giving and gracefully receiving constructive feedback.
- Focusing critique on the code and the argument, not the person who wrote
  it.
- Assuming good faith, especially in a bug report or a design disagreement.

Examples of behavior that does not:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Publishing someone else's private information — an address, a workplace,
  anything not already public — without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of an issue, a pull request, or a discussion.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every space this project maintains — issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other channel where the project is being represented —
and in any other setting where someone's conduct is understood to represent
this project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to **runboundai@gmail.com**, the same
address [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) uses for private reports. Include what
happened and where; a link to the issue, comment, or thread is enough. We
will review every report, respond, and take whatever action fits — a
private word, a public correction, or removal from the project's spaces —
proportionate to what happened. Reports are handled in confidence.
